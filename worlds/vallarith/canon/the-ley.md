# The Ley

Vanta's ley document is the authority on how magic works. This page restates it for play and adds
what it means at the table. Where the older lorebook disagrees, this page follows the ley document
and notes the difference.

## What the Ley is

- **A current, not a mind.** The Ley is an invisible field of power running under the world in
  channels called Ley Lines. It does not watch, judge, remember or choose. It is what was left
  when the Creator Dragons turned themselves into the world.
- **Fonts** are places where the current rises near the surface. Magic is stronger near them.
- **The Source Font** is where creation began, where sun and moon first collapsed into matter and
  time. Every line and Font is downstream of it. It cannot be drained. Damming it does not use it
  up; it stops the current reaching everyone else. Mortal tradition puts it in the mountains above
  Loch Torogh, where the Ley does rise strongest (see `hidden-truths.json` for where it really is
  in the Post-Reform era).

## Casting

Ordinary magic borrows from the local current, shapes it, and lets it go; it flows back and nothing
is lost. How well it works depends on two things:

1. **Local density.** Close to a line or Font, magic is strong and dependable. Far from one, it is
   weak and unreliable.
2. **The caster.** Attunement, training and the body's limits set how much can be drawn safely.
   Drawing past that hurts, and can kill.

Gods, dragons and great casters carry reservoirs that store Ley for a while. A reservoir lets them
keep casting; it does not make them stronger.

## Crystallized Ley

Where the current gets denser than the world can circulate, it hardens into crystal: veins,
nodules and growths in stone. Ley mines are scars, not gifts.

- Crystal is power taken out of the current for good. Spent or shattered, most of it is lost,
  thrown off violently or scattered.
- Mining crystal thins the magic of the land around the mine, permanently. Old mining country is
  unstable and dangerous.
- Crystal makes **predictability** possible. Stored, portable power lets a city keep wards, lights,
  healing houses, transit and industry running where the natural flow would not allow it.

That gives three kinds of place:

| Kind | Relation to crystal | When the supply fails | Examples in this draft |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Ley-rich** | Natural flow is strong; crystal is seen as decadent or dangerous | Little changes | Gurdalur Stones, Rulan Gaan, the Heartwood at Kingswood, Petrakyra, the Badlands heart |
| **Ley-adaptive** | Uses some crystal to make up for thin flow | Decline, unrest, migration | Dun Aldun, Cyrel, Mendle, Eriane |
| **Ley-engineered** | Cannot function without stored power | Collapse in a cascade | Trein, Asania, the Obirykan City States |

Wrenspire is the odd one out: thin flow, and it chose machines instead of crystal.

## Ascension

Ordinary Fonts never make anyone a god, however long they stay. The Source Font is different.
Getting close to it exposes a being to the conditions before limits and identity applied.

- For almost everyone, the result is coming apart into raw Ley and rejoining the current without a
  trace.
- For a very few, with extraordinary attunement, the right make-up and preparation, the exposure
  rewrites what they are and how much power they can hold. That is ascension.
- Survival is not the same as success. Some come out fractured, unstable, or turned into things
  that are neither mortal nor divine.

Gor'gel is the exception that proves the rule: he did it at Ghondiir's Font, not the Source, with a
dragon's heart-blood and a blade of crystallized Ley, which is why no one has managed to repeat it.

## In play

- **Density is a modifier.** Each region and settlement has a `ley` rating (`rich`, `normal`,
  `thin`, `dead`). The Referee shifts Essence rolls by it.
- **Crystal is fuel and contraband.** Burning a crystal gives a big one-off boost and marks
  **Ley scarring** (a dark track). Crystal prices swing with mine output, war and Brotherhood policy.
- **The brooch.** Under the old Dam, a Brotherhood brooch was a licence to draw regulated current.
  In the present the Ley flows free, but Trein is reintroducing licences, and the brooch is coming
  back as both a permit and a leash.
- **Nothing here is moral.** Spells do not hurt the world; interrupting circulation does. Damming,
  mining and hoarding have costs that pile up over decades.

## Where the lorebook differs

- The lorebook says the Creator Dragons sleep and will wake at the end to take their power back.
  The ley document says they spent themselves completely and nothing of their minds survives. This
  draft treats the ley document as true and the sleeping-dragons story as a belief held by mortals
  and by the Eternal Dragons themselves (see `hidden-truths.json`).
- The lorebook calls the current "the Ley Line" (singular). The Ley Line map shows one great line
  running north to south along the eastern mountains, with branch currents. This draft uses "the
  Ley" for the whole system and "the Great Line" for the main channel.
