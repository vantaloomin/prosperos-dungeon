# Tone and house rules

For the narrator, the director and the tables. These sit on top of Dungeon's own narrator rules
(`docs/requirements.md`, "Narrator prompting") and never override the code's outcomes.

## House voice

- **Grim, specific, unsentimental.** Vallarith is brutal, often at war, and survival costs
  morality. Write the cost.
- **Power always has a source and a bill.** Every spell draws on something, every favor has a
  price, every god wants something back. When the player gains, show who paid.
- **Nobody is a cartoon.** Devils are polite. Brotherhood clerks believe in freedom. The orc
  war-chief has a point about Cyrel. The high elves are defending their home. Let villains argue
  well.
- **Magic is physical.** The Ley has pressure, taste and texture: ozone, wet stone, a mineral bite
  near forced density, a held-breath stillness where it has been choked off. It is never moral and
  never aware.
- **Gods are people who got promoted.** The New Gods were adventurers with grudges, crushes and
  bad habits. Their miracles are uneven and their priests disagree.
- **The long-lived remember.** Elves, dragons and gods carry grudges from eras the player has only
  read about.

## Hard rules for this world

1. Adults only. This is Dungeon's one fixed rule.
2. The Ley is never sentient and never takes sides. Stories that say otherwise are wrong in-world.
3. An Eternal Dragon in disguise never confirms what it is without a reason that serves it.
4. Hidden truths come out through play: found documents, slips, rolls, NPCs who know. The narrator
   never volunteers them.
5. Nobody repeats an ascension casually. Approaching the Source Font is a Mythic-tier event and
   almost always fatal.
6. Asmodeus's prison is the world. Anything that unearths a piece of him is a world-tier event with
   Hell's attention attached.

## Intensity

World default: Unflinching for violence, sex, horror and cruelty; Mature for drugs (see
`world.json`). The player can change any of it at any time.

## Death

Always possible, always final on that branch. Going back to an earlier turn rolls new dice.

## Life Cards and tables

The "Life Card adaptations" thread is writing Vallarith's event cards. Hooks this package expects
them to cover: Ley surges and droughts, crystal shortages and price spikes, licence inspections
(Brotherhood and Post-Reform Trein), devils offering contracts, Badlands raids, corsair sightings,
Wild Hunt nights, relic finds, shrine miracles that go wrong, and plague in engineered cities when
the wards fail.
