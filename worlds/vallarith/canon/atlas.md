# Atlas

A walk around the map, north to south. Structured data for every place is in `regions.json` and
`settlements.json`; the Ley ratings come from the Ley Line map.

## The Great Line

The main Ley channel runs north to south along the eastern mountains. It rises out of the northern
sea, passes just east of Rulan Gaan and the peaks above Loch Torogh (where tradition puts the
Source Font), follows the mountain wall down to the heart of the Badlands, and bends south-east
through the eastern forests into the sea past Ikto Isle. Branch currents fan out from two
convergences:

- **The northern convergence**, by Rulan Gaan, sends a branch east across the Dire Sea to the
  Gurdalur Stones.
- **The Badlands convergence**, where Ghondiir died, sends branches west toward Kingswood and
  Convila, south-west into the Badlands, and east into the forests toward Petrakyra.

Everything west of the mountains (Trein, Asania, Wrenspire, Mordun, Brynwyr, the islands) is thin.
That is why the west runs on crystal, and why Wrenspire built machines.

## The north: the Dwarven Empire

- **Dun Aldun**, the Empire's mountain capital under the Stoneheart Clan. Its smiths, led from the
  shop of Master Grimm, make most of the world's enchanted arms and armor. Few outsiders get past
  the gates.
- **Mordun**, a dwarf-built city full of humans, on the Gulf of Aldir. The trade door into the
  Empire, harried by pirates.
- **Rulan Gaan**, a swamp in the frozen north, made by the Eternal black dragon Vymnommyth and held
  by his dwarven cult. Outsiders are turned away, or worse.
- **Loch Torogh**, a dwarven fishing and trading town on a great lake below the mountains where the
  New Gods ascended.
- **Mendle**, a small town between the human and dwarven lands that punches far above its size as
  the place where treaties get signed.

## The Gulf of Aldir and the Scullcrossed Islands

A calm gulf between Trein and Mordun, crowded with laden ships. In the middle sit the Scullcrossed
Islands, home of the Dreadfall Corsairs. Few survive them, and the gulf's merchants are in a
permanent arms race with them.

## The west: the Kingdoms of Man

- **Trein**, the city of magic and home of the Brotherhood of Man. Lit streets, enchanted everything,
  and a total dependence on stored Ley. Friendly with Asania, careful to stay out of Cyrel's wars.
- **Asania**, capital of its kingdom on the Asanian Strait: marble towers, magic on every corner,
  and an endless war with Cyrel. Allied with Trein's sorcerers, wary of Wrenspire.
- **Wrenspire**, a cliff-top city-state of gnome and halfling engineers run by a council, building
  machines and airships without magic.
- **Cyrel**, the fortress capital of a kingdom of three sister cities, at war with Asania to the
  west and the Badlands to the east.
  - **Eriane**, on a lake: farms, vineyards and breweries, the kingdom's larder, full of humans and
    halflings.
  - **Kingswood**, the logging town on the Brynwyr edge where the slice is set. Rumored to sit on a
    Ley convergence (it does: the Heartwood).
  - **Convila**, a busy town on the ruins of an older city, over a spent Ley fountain sealed by the
    old Convila Pact.
  - **Bastion**, the great fortress facing the Badlands.
  - **Mossrun**, the Ilvar family's company mining town in the wet valleys north-east of Cyrel,
    under the Great Line: iron and Ley crystal, dying moss, metallic streams.

## The Badlands

The country Ghondiir's fall made: broken mountains, dry basins and dead trees. Three tribes hold it,
the Dramogon gnolls in the north, the Unguruk orcs in the middle, and the Kadragar goblins on the
southern coast. Unguruk holds the ruin of **Adlmyr**, the Cyrel fortress it took before the Brotherhood
was founded. West of it stands the **Abandoned Keep**, which locals call the Druid's Keep: Himo
Xiloscient's ward-keep over the door the horde came through in 251 AOD, empty since Adlmyr fell. Under all of it, the Badlands
convergence: some of the richest Ley on the continent, under the most dangerous ground.

## The south-west: Brynwyr and Sa'ja'een Bay

- **Brynwyr**, the high elves' forest realm on the south-western peninsula, with sylvan cities and
  sea-elf fleets. At war with Cyrel's loggers over every tree. Its young sometimes wander into
  human lands.
- **Sa'ja'een Bay**, a glowing bay of coral towers, silver mist and night-singing creatures.

## The southern seas

- **The Sea of Kujuur** wraps the south: sunken wealth, rough water, goblin ships.
- **Bashrat Island**, a steaming swamp of singing creatures and stranger people, with a famous
  market and the village of **Ota'at**.
- **Ikto Isle**, past the Mi Cho Rapids, home of the **Benzaiten Monastery**, where monks of many
  peoples train mind and body, and which alone keeps open contact with Petrakyra.

## The east

- **Petrakyra**, the hidden wood-elf home between sea, forest and mountain. Rumor says an Eternal
  Dragon lives among them. Rumor is right.
- **Etryona**, an island across the Kyrana Channel, home of the **Obirykan City States**, where Ley
  crystal is money and power is counted in carats.
- **The Dire Sea**, between the continent and Fjallagracht. The Ley branch to the Gurdalur Stones
  runs under it and throws up Ley squalls, storms with glowing fog, and crystal reefs grow along
  the current. Only Fjallagracht longships cross, in the summer weeks the stones allow.
- **The Fjallagracht Lands**, a frozen island of three clans off the north-east coast across the Dire Sea,
  home of the **Gurdalur Stones**, a ring of ancient standing stones on a Ley branch that set the
  calendar of the people who live there.
