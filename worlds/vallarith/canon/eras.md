# The three campaign eras

Vanta's call (2026-10-21): Vallarith is one world package with three times a campaign can start
in. The map, the peoples and most cities stay the same across all three, because the Reformation
rebuilt the world in the image of the old one. What changes is who holds power, how magic is
reached, and which gods answer.

Entries elsewhere in this package carry an `eras` list (missing means all three) and a `by_era`
object where a fact changes. The importer can build one world file with an era setting, or one
file per era.

## Pre-Reform (about 2750 AOM)

The Age of Magic, about 230 years before the Brotherhood is founded (Vanta, 2026-10-21: two
centuries earlier changes little about the cities).

- **The Ley flows free and unlicensed.** Anyone who can touch it can use it. Crystal mining is a
  trade, not a policy, and Trein and Asania are already leaning on it.
- **The Old Gods are fading but not gone.** Shrines still get faint answers. Priests argue about
  why the answers are getting fainter.
- **No New Gods.** Tidum is an ancient, deathless archdruid walking the forests. The others are not
  born yet.
- **The Brotherhood does not exist yet.** Mistress Sara is in Trein, cultivating scholars and
  merchants who resent temples and dragons. The Brotherhood will be founded in 2984.
- **Adlmyr stands.** Cyrel's border fortress still holds the Badlands line beside Bastion. It will
  fall to the tribes before the Brotherhood is founded.
- **The Druid's Keep is garrisoned.** Elves and Cyrel soldiers take turns holding the ward-keep
  Himo Xiloscient built over the door of 251 AOD.
- **Hell is far away.** The Blood War is a story about other planes.
- **Good for:** classic sword-and-sorcery, Old God cults, dragon intrigue, seeing the world the
  Brotherhood will strangle.

## The Brotherhood Years (3001 to 3135 BOM, default start 3121)

The Dam is up. The default start sits in the 3120s, as the "pre-Invasion rot" sets in and the
future New Gods are still mortal adventurers.

- **Magic is licensed.** Away from the Fonts the Ley is almost gone, and what flows is regulated.
  A Brotherhood brooch is legal permission to cast. Unlicensed casting is a crime, and in thin
  regions it barely works anyway.
- **Cities run on Brotherhood crystal.** Engineered cities live on shipments, and the ledgers
  decide which districts go dark. Poor quarters lose their wards and healers first.
- **The Brotherhood rules in the open** from Trein, with Mistress Sara at its head.
- **Devils are signing deals.** Strangers in clean robes at war-fires, corpses with contracts burned
  into their wrists, elementals going silent near certain stones.
- **The future New Gods are on the road:** Nicana, Girione and Aveus first, then Cybelia (a senior
  Brotherhood wizard), Tidum, Witarr (a grave-cleric working out of Convila) and the Arbiter. They
  can be met, allied with, or played (see `personas.json`).
- **The Invasion is coming.** 3129 brings the Demon Invasion; 3136 brings the Reformation. A
  campaign in this era can run into the end of the world.
- **Good for:** resistance against a magical bureaucracy, smuggling crystal and licences, devil
  bargains, playing alongside or as the future gods.

## Post-Reform (about 300 RFM, the default)

About 300 years after the Reformation. This is the lorebook's present and the default campaign
era. Kingswood, the Milestone 1 slice town, is set here.

- **The Ley flows free again,** but Trein is quietly bringing back licences.
- **The New Gods are young.** Their shrines are a few centuries old, their priesthoods squabble, and
  their miracles are uneven. The Arbiter is unknown to mortals.
- **The old Brotherhood is a half-remembered scandal,** and Mistress Sara has revived it in secret,
  its first rising in centuries.
- **Adlmyr has fallen** to the Unguruk tribe, which holds the ruin.
- **Asmodeus is buried** in eight pieces under the world. Hell's cults on the surface are looking
  for them.
- **Relics from before the Reformation** are rare and priced like saints' bones.
- **Good for:** the default grimdark campaign, from Kingswood quarrels up to dragons, archdevils
  and the Source Font.

## What changes between eras

| Thing | Pre-Reform | Brotherhood Years | Post-Reform |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Reaching the Ley | free | licensed by brooch; thin away from Fonts | free; Trein reintroducing licences |
| Gods who answer | Old Gods, faintly | Old Gods barely; none new | New Gods; Old Gods silent |
| Brotherhood of Man | not yet founded | rules openly from Trein | secret revival |
| Adlmyr | Cyrel fortress (falls before 2984) | Unguruk-held ruin | Unguruk-held ruin |
| The Abandoned Keep | garrisoned ward-keep | abandoned | abandoned, wards failing |
| Asmodeus | rules from Nessus | leads the Invasion (3129) | buried in eight pieces; throne vacant |
| New Gods' party | Tidum only, as a mortal archdruid | mortal adventurers | gods |
| Mossrun and the Ilvars | do not exist | do not exist | fifth Ilvar patriarch |
| Kingswood's Shrine of Tidum | no | no | yes |
