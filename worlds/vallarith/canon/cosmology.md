# Cosmology

Names follow [names.md](names.md): figures from myth keep their names, D&D-coined ones are renamed.

## The order of powers

1. **The Creator Dragons**, Iseullaryax of the Sun and Tatsuyaraga of the Moon, made the world and
   became it. What is left of them is the Ley.
2. **The Eternal Dragons** were given a share of the Creators' power. They outlast ordinary dragons
   by a long way, most live disguised as mortals, and none of them can breed any more.
3. **The Old Gods** took their strength from the Eternals, ruled the Material Plane, and faded into
   realms of their own making. They are missing.
4. **The New Gods** are seven mortals who ascended at the Source Font during the Demon Invasion and
   then remade the world. They are a few centuries old in the Post-Reform era.
5. **Outside powers:** the courts of Faerie, the Nine Hells and the Abyss. They are older than the
   New Gods and do not answer to them.

Ghondiir, God King of Dragons, sits outside this list: the one Eternal who was ever killed. His
body made the Badlands, and his killer, Gor'gel, became the first mortal to ascend and the war god
of the Old pantheon.

## The Material Plane as a prison

At the Reformation the New Gods tore Asmodeus into eight pieces, buried them deep under the
world with the heart lowest, and rebuilt the world around them as a living prison. Mortals know
the Reformation as the day the world was unmade and made again; the prison part is a hidden
truth. The world's shape, its Fonts and its gods are, in part, the bars and the wardens.

## Faerie

A reflection of the world soaked in magic: forests and mountains brighter and stranger than the
real ones, ruled by rules that contradict each other, and caught between dream and nightmare. Beauty
and danger are the same thing there.

- **Titania**, the Seelie Queen: summer, growth, light, art. Generous until scorned.
- **Mab**, the Unseelie Queen, Queen of Air and Darkness: winter, cold, decay that feeds rebirth.
  She meddles in mortal affairs for her own reasons.
- The two queens are sisters, equal in power, and have not spoken in ages. Which one is stronger at
  a given moment depends on who holds the Material Plane's solstice.
- **The Leanan Sídhe**, a muse who belongs to neither court. Her gift is great art; her price is
  obsession, heartbreak and usually an early grave.
- **The Erlking**, lord of the Wild Hunt and of the spirits of dead hunters, outside both courts.
  He rides with black hounds on the wind. He is also the God King of Goblins.

## The Nine Hells

A ladder of nine layers, each ruled by an archdevil, held together by law, contract and promotion
through treachery. Devils buy souls with power and want the Material Plane bound by pacts.

| Layer | Character | Ruler |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Avernus | Burning battlefield, front line of the Blood War | Bel the Warlord, who overthrew Azazel |
| Dis | Iron city of industry and paranoia | Dispater, the Iron Duke, with his consort Proserpine |
| Acheron | A city on a sinking swamp of greed | Mammon the Viscount |
| Phlegethos | Volcanic wastes and rivers of fire | Agrat, with her father Belial behind her |
| Stygia | Frozen sea and ice-locked fortresses | Andras, frozen inside a mountain of ice |
| Malebolge | Ground that shifts and lies | Naamah, daughter of Asmodeus |
| Lethe | Ruins of grand works, labyrinth fortresses | Beelzebub, the Slug Archduke |
| Caina | Mountains of ice and cold intellect | Mephistopheles |
| Nessus | The deepest layer, the Black Citadel | Asmodeus, King of the Nine Hells (see below) |

**The fiction of the throne.** In every era before the Reformation, Asmodeus rules from the Black
Citadel. After it, the Hells keep up the story that he still does. Who actually sits there is an
open question for Vanta.

## The Abyss

Endless layers of chaos, each shaped by whichever demon lord holds it. Demons want to consume and
corrupt, and in the end to unmake creation itself.

| Lord | Domain |
| --- | --- |
| Demogorgon, Prince of Demons | The Gaping Maw, a storm-ocean of wrecks and nightmare islands |
| Orcus, Lord of Undeath | Thanatos, the 113th layer, frozen necropolis country |
| Belphegor, Prince of Indulgence | The Gilded Layers (45th to 47th), beautiful cities in tortured land |
| Arachne, the Spinner | The Webbed Deeps, tunnels and caverns of web |
| Baphomet, the Horned King | The Endless Maze, the 600th layer |
| Haggoth, the Gnoll Lord | Roams the Infinite Layers; patron of the Dramogon gnolls |

## The Blood War

Devils and demons have fought each other forever. They stopped once, for the Demon Invasion of
3129, to use the Material Plane as a road into the Celestial worlds. The Reformation ended that.
