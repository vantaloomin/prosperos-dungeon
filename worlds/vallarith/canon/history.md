# History of Vallarith

Years run on from the death of Ghondiir without resetting. Each era gets its own suffix (AOD,
AOM, BOM, RFM). Where the source is silent, this file says so rather than filling the gap; the
gaps are listed in [open-questions.md](../open-questions.md).

## Before the count: creation and the Primordial Orcs

The twin Creator Dragons, Iseullaryax of the Sun and Tatsuyaraga of the Moon, made the world and
spent themselves doing it. What was left of their power became the Ley. Before they were gone they
handed some of that power to their chosen: the Eternal Dragons, and through them, the Old Gods.

The first people were the Primordial Orcs, few in number and slaves to the dragons. Their master
was Ghondiir, a vast chromatic dragon and God King of Dragons, who ruled dragonkind by force and
used mortals for labor, comfort and spectacle. His lair sat on the largest Ley Font on the
continent.

## Age of Dragons (1 to 750 AOD)

- **Before the count.** No Eternal clutch has hatched since Ghondiir died; the dragons do not say
  why (see `hidden-truths.json`).
- **1 AOD, the death of Ghondiir.** A Primordial Orc named Gor'gel, called the Perfect Warrior by
  his tribe, climbed the tallest mountain with an axe whose blade was a glowing blue crystal. The
  fight lasted hours and rained blood and magic off the peak. Gor'gel tore out the Dragon King's
  heart. Between the heart's blood and his crystal blade he became the first mortal to ascend.
  He threw the body down; the impact broke the mountains and left the scarred country now called
  the Badlands. Gor'gel shared his new power with his people. Orcs say he gave half of them his
  strength and half his cunning, and so made orcs and humans. Others say humans and orcs grew from
  the Dragon King's blood and bones. Both stories agree the two peoples are kin.
- **151 AOD, the Badlands Migration.** Humans leave the Badlands, driven out by orc wars, and settle
  the western lands that become the Kingdoms of Man.
- **251 AOD, Ohgnu's Invasion,** also called the Gor'gel Deception. Gor'gel, disguised as another
  god, dupes the elf druid Himo Xiloscient into opening the first portal. Gnolls and demons pour
  through, with orc and goblin war bands behind them. Himo makes his stand at Kingswood to stop
  what he let in, breaks the host, and builds a ward-keep over the door west of Adlmyr. Kingswood
  still tells it as Xiloscient's stand, and leaves out that he was an elf and the door was his.
- **751 to 1000.** No record.

## Age of Magic (1001 to 3000 AOM)

Two thousand years the source names but does not describe. By its end the Ley is in wide use,
Fonts are known, cities have learned to mine crystallized Ley, and the Old Gods are fading into
their own realms.

- **About 2750 AOM,** the Pre-Reform campaign era opens.
- **Before 2984, the fall of Adlmyr.** The Badlands tribes break Cyrel's border fortress, and the
  Unguruk tribe takes the ruin. The Druid's Keep west of it is abandoned.

## Age of Man (2984 to 3135 BOM)

- **2984 BOM, the founding of the Brotherhood of Man.** In Trein, a populist movement argues that
  magic should belong to mortals, not to gods, dragons or temples. Scholars, merchants, mages and
  the disaffected join. Behind it stands Mistress Sara, who is the Eternal White Dragon
  Saradrosk.
- **3001 BOM, the Damming of the Ley.** The Brotherhood seizes the Ley Font and chokes the current
  at its source. Magic away from the Fonts thins to almost nothing, and the Brotherhood licenses
  what flows: a brooch on your coat is legal permission to draw regulated current. Cities that
  need stored power become dependent on Brotherhood crystal shipments. Poor districts lose their
  wards first.
- **The 3120s, the first signs.** Devils turn up at war-fires offering deals. Corpses come back with
  contracts burned into their wrists. Elementals go silent near certain stones. The choke points the
  Brotherhood built in the Ley turn out to make perfect gates for anything that understands
  contracts. A party forms around the monk Nicana, the halfling bard Girione and the half-orc
  Aveus, and later takes in the Brotherhood wizard Cybelia, the archdruid Tidum, the tiefling
  grave-cleric Witarr, and a stranger called the Arbiter.
- **3129 BOM, the Demon Invasion.** Hell and the Abyss, Law and Chaos, set aside the Blood War for
  one purpose: to overrun the Material Plane and use it as the road into the Celestial worlds,
  with Asmodeus at the head. The Brotherhood's grip shatters.
- **The ascension.** Cybelia works out how to draw on the Ley Font directly. At the Font in the
  mountains by Loch Torogh, the party enters it and survives as gods.

## Reformation (3136 RFM onward)

- **3136 RFM, the World Reformation.** The New Gods put every mortal into stasis and destroyed the
  world. They tore Asmodeus into eight pieces (two arms, two legs, his torso, his head, his heart
  and his Ruby Rod), buried them far under the earth with the heart deepest of all, and remade the
  world around them as a living prison. Then they woke everyone up.
- **After.** The Ley flows free again. The old Brotherhood is broken and scattered. The Eternal
  Dragons go back to their disguises. Lady Zee begins collecting what survived.
- **Early in the Reformation era** (this draft puts it near 20 RFM, five Ilvar lives ago), the Mossrun Expedition
  strikes two veins of ore. Its leader, Aldon Ilvar, is murdered and replaced by Zaraxas, who has
  worn the Ilvar name ever since.
- **Post-Reform present** (about 300 RFM, the default campaign era). Melton Ilvar is the fifth Ilvar
  patriarch. Mistress Sara has revived the Brotherhood in Trein for the first time in centuries.
  Cyrel and Asania are at war again. Unguruk holds Adlmyr. Kingswood's loggers are about to cross
  the treaty stones into the Heartwood.

The three campaign eras are described in [eras.md](eras.md).
