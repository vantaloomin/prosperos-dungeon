# Open questions

Calls this draft makes where the archive is silent or missing a file, and what is still open. Mabry
Curve marks many of its own facts as proposed; they are canon unless contradicted, and this package
treats them that way.

## Settled for this package

- **Where a campaign starts:** arrival night, on the Concourse (and the Gilded Bazaar for Bri and
  Chase). Mabry Curve's later events are planned beats and hidden truths, not history.
- **Captain Kael is Captain Berit Hode** here (Vanta's naming rule). Mabry Curve keeps Kael.
- **Minors are left out of the cast.** Mabry Curve has two Keep teenagers (a serving girl of
  seventeen and a sixteen-year-old rider); this package does not ship them as NPCs.
- **The explicit off-page record** of Bri and Chase is summarized as hidden truths and beats; the
  verbatim scenes stay in Vanta's archive.

## Drafted here (origin: draft)

- The **market rows** as the faction that collects the Concourse's stall rents (Hamid owes
  "whoever controls the market rows").
- **The Shut** as Sister Pia's order, from the Archdeacon's "The Shut keeps it. I will speak to her
  Mother-house."
- **The stilt villages** as a settlement on the Marches, and the place names on the town files that
  the story doesn't give (Concourse streets, Hollin lanes, Bazaar rows). Each is marked draft.
- **Ages** for NPCs the story doesn't age (Hamid, Ruth Ann, Pia, Coll, the Margrave and others).
- **MARVEL stats** for Bri, Chase and the NPCs. Grant's and Kenzie's are Vanta's base sheets at
  creation.
- **Tier mapping** of BABEL's ranks onto Dungeon's ladder: Unranked to E Local, D and C Regional,
  B and A Realm, S and SS World, SSS and Ultra Mythic.
- **Travel times:** about half a day's climb per floor on the Stair; an hour by cage to Varrow.
