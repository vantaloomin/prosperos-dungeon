# The four from Halvorsen's

The playable personas, and what the world does with whichever of them the player is not. Their full
entries (with what each one hides) are in `cast.json`; each persona's starting sheet is in
`personas.json` and the town premade files.

## The night of the arrival

Saturday, October 17, 2026, a storm sitting over Round Rock like a bruise. The Mabrys and the Coles
have taken the back room of Halvorsen's Chophouse, under a longhorn skull the size of a war shield,
to bless an engagement. Grant wants to be anywhere else and is crunching for the BABEL reveal on
November 13. Chase comes in half an hour late through the side door. Kenzie is in a black dress
for once. Rachel Ostrowski, five weeks on medical leave, messages Grant out of nowhere, with
"recieve" spelled the way she always spells it.

About a quarter to ten, in the hallway by the coat check, Chase kisses Bri, and Kenzie sees it. Then
the lights go.

## The pairs

- **Grant Mabry** (34) and **Kenzie Cole** (20) come down together on the Concourse, on Fire Market
  morning, into the wreck of Hamid's skewer cart. Grant is a half-built eight-foot Nephil with a
  torn knee and white rings in his eyes; Kenzie is an Auric she never asked for, every tattoo gone,
  with two gin and tonics in her satchel.
- **Bri Cole** (26) and **Chase Whitaker** (34) come down on the Gilded Bazaar, three floors up,
  not on the Concourse Roll. Bri is Human and the only one of the four whose class request was
  honored (Breaker). Chase is a wolf-line Therian whose Climber request was not.

## Before the tower

- The couple's sharing fantasy was only ever talk, with no rules. Grant told Chase about it; Bri
  told Kenzie, last Christmas, as a brag.
- Bri used sex as currency in her past and wants it rougher than Grant has ever dared; she wants to
  be seen fully.
- Kenzie has spent her life as the rival sister, the prettier one under a hoodie, and wants Grant,
  and Bri, without knowing it.
- Chase wants to be as good as Grant and to have what is his, and he asks "That cool?" after.
- Something happened between Kenzie and Chase on July 4, 2025.

## When a persona isn't the player

The others act on their own every turn (Vanta's rule for Mabry Curve, and Dungeon's for every
NPC): what they want right now, whether they make a move on the player or on the story, and how
their own threads advance. They can be influenced like real people, never commanded. What they do
off the page goes to the off-page beats and reaches the player only in a scene: through Rachel, a
System pop-up, evidence, a confession, or catching them.

## Rachel

Rachel Ostrowski (31), Nth Floor's live-ops lead, is not a persona: she is the Usher. She sees every
scene on every floor, wanted Grant for years and said nothing, and doesn't know what she wants now.
