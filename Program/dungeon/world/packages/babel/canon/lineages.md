# Lineages

The races of the creation pane, as the tower builds them, and the native lineages climbers meet.
Mabry Curve's `lineages.md` (bodies, tells, Long House names, breeding, Advanced lineages) was not
in the archive this package was built from; this is what the story files establish, and the gaps
are listed in [../open-questions.md](../open-questions.md).

The tower rebuilds an arrival's body to match the race it grants, and the rebuild can freeze
partway (`[Condition: Unfinished]`).

| Lineage | Lean | What it does to you | Tells |

## Natives

- **The Lit.** Varrow's church is run by Seraphs: ash-gray wings barred with white, a dim halo,
  pale fire at the pupils, skin lit faintly along the bones. Their vowed show light along the
  collarbones as they near Consecration. A Seraph's spoken word is obeyed; a Seraph who bends his
  own word pays for it in a stuttering halo and a molted feather, and enough bent vows make him
  Fall. A Seraph sworn to a partner bears Nephil.
- **Lilim.** Spoken of on the Bazaar and never admitted to.
- **Reach giants.** Gray, twice a big man's height, long-armed, heads set low; a language of their
  own.

## The Watcher Trace

The Watcher admits one holder at a time and needs a bound partner to watch through. When its holder
stops climbing, the lineage is **vacated**. An arrival who reaches for a vacated Watcher with no
Tether gets a **Trace**: the white ring, flashes of sight through other eyes, and a residue that sits
upstream of anything else the tower builds in them. The Trace is ancestral to the Nephil and can
override that pick, leaving it unstable.

## Hidden in the lineages

What the Close knows and climbers don't: the Reach giants are Nephil gone feral, unbound and unvowed
and grown past any cap; a Close gift is a giant bound to a sister and set on the Wall against his
own kind. See `hidden-truths.json` (`the-reach-are-nephil`, `the-scales-read-the-tether`).
