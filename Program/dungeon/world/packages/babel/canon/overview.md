# BABEL

At their engagement party in the back room of Halvorsen's Chophouse in Round Rock, Texas, on a
stormy Saturday night in October 2026, software engineer Grant Mabry, his fiancée Bri Cole, his
best friend Chase Whitaker and Bri's younger sister Kenzie Cole are pulled out of the world. The
lights go out, and they wake bodiless in the dark in front of a creation screen set in the typeface
Grant's studio paid forty thousand dollars for.

They are in a real tower of themed floors that has dressed itself up as BABEL, the unreleased
full-dive MMORPG Grant helped build at Nth Floor. The game's systems are his systems, until they
aren't. The tower has taken people in waves for generations and wears a different disguise for each
one; this wave it wears his.

## What makes it BABEL

- **A tower of floors.** Each floor runs on its own tropes: a fantasy kingdom with a church that
  has teeth, a fen of stilt villages, a merchant city where everything has a price, a steampunk
  foundry, a hospital where it is always night. One shaft, the Stair, runs through all of them.
  Moving between floors strips or changes what you carry. See [the-tower.md](the-tower.md).
- **A System that lies by leaving things out.** Status windows, ranks, classes and skills, the
  Mabry curve, Waystones, satchels: BABEL's onboarding, nearly word for word. But selection is a
  request, lives are hidden, and fields you can't read yet are garbled. See
  [the-system.md](the-system.md).
- **Desire is power.** Taboo, emotional bonds, power exchange, betrayal and broken vows pay power
  the System never advertises. It changes the user and binds the partner. Tether, the bond system
  Grant designed and cut, is back, and it no longer needs both partners to agree.
- **Natives and climbers.** The tower's natives live real lives on their floors with a lesser
  System of their own. Arrivals are climbers: they don't need to eat, plain food tastes of ash to
  them, and the natives call them the Unhungry. Veterans of earlier waves remember the old skin.
- **Someone inside is watching.** The voice at the creation screen, the Usher, is Rachel
  Ostrowski, Grant's live-ops lead, trapped in the System. She can nudge, and every nudge costs
  her a piece of herself.

## The cast

The four from Halvorsen's are the playable personas, and whoever the player is not stays in the
world as an autonomous person with their own wants. See [the-four.md](the-four.md) and
`cast.json`. Grant and Kenzie come down together on the Concourse. Bri and Chase come down three
floors up, on the Gilded Bazaar, unranked on a D floor, in the one city built on pricing bodies.

## Where a campaign starts

On arrival night: the four land as the tower first sets them down. The
Mabry Curve roleplay is the shape of what tends to happen next (`beats.json`), and its secrets are
this package's hidden truths. Nothing after the landing is fixed.

## Commitments

Adult cast only. Explicit content on the page. A world of consequence, impact and failure: two
steps forward, one back. Built-in triggers for love, lust, sex and taboo: sharing, family by
marriage, power exchange, dubious consent as fantasy. See
[tone-and-house-rules.md](tone-and-house-rules.md).

Inspirations: Solo Leveling, Overgeared, LitRPG and GameLit, isekai.
