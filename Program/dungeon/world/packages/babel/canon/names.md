# Names

Every name in Mabry Curve is Vanta's canon, and this package keeps them, with one change.

| In Mabry Curve | In this package | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Captain Berit Kael | Captain Berit Hode | "Kael" is on Vanta's list of AI-trope names (project memory, naming preferences) |

Names invented here to fill gaps follow the same rule: plain, period-sounding, slightly odd, never
the stock names (Voss, Maren, Thorne, Kael, Elara) or "the Drowned X" inns. New ones are marked
`origin: draft` where they appear.

## How people are named

- **Climbers** keep their Earth names, and the Roll records where they came from (`GRANT MABRY ·
  Earth · Nephil`).
- **Varrow** names run old English and rustic: Marra, Aldo, Coll, Tamsin, Abelone, Maud, Hask, Gil
  Fenwick, Pim, Tobe Ashby, Wat Cooper, Orla, Hennet, Oswy, Edmund and Aveline Corve.
- **The Bazaar** is mixed and mercantile: Sabeen Oro, Okonkwo Vale, Pell the Wire, Old Wick, Nell.
- **The Lit** go by office (the Archdeacon, the Bishop) more than by name.
