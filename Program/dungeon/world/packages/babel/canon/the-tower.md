# The tower

## The shape of it

A tower of floors stacked on one shaft. Nobody agrees what is at the top. Floor Zero, the
Concourse, is the hub where arrivals land; floors are numbered up and down from it. Each floor
runs on its own tropes, and tech, items and even magic can fail when you cross from one to
another.

Twenty-eight more floors existed in BABEL's design documents, including a pleasure floor that
never reached the build. The tower may have all of them, or none, or others. Firearms work on Dry
Hollow, Lumen Stack and the Ashfields.

## The Stair

One shaft as wide as a stadium runs the height of the tower, so old the hands of climbers have
polished the stone. A cart-wide stair spirals its inner wall; an iron lift cage rides chains up
the middle. People live on it: a man with a mule and two baskets of cabbages, women in red
carrying something long wrapped in a sheet, a boy on a step with his feet over the drop.

- **The cage** from the Concourse's east gate goes one floor up (Varrow) and one floor down (St.
  Agathe's). It only reaches Waystones you have touched. Past that, you walk.
- **Floor gates.** Some floors' landings have their own gates and tolls. The Bazaar's, behind the
  Copper Steps, belongs to Sabeen Oro: two Talents up, down free, because nobody comes back to pay.
- **The broken span.** Above the Foundry's landing a whole span of the Stair has fallen into the
  shaft, leaving bolt holes across a gap nobody could jump. Whoever goes higher goes in through the
  floor and finds its own way up.
- **Private stairs.** Varrow Keep's bone-cellar hides a stair onto the shaft that goes both up and
  down, bypassing the cage. The Margrave sells passage on it.
- **The night cage.** A smugglers' crew runs an unlicensed cargo cage down the chains from the
  Bazaar's freight wharf.
- In BABEL each floor's stair was its own dungeon. In the tower those per-floor stairs, if they
  exist as the build had them, hang off the one Stair.

## Transitions

Every move between floors reviews what you carry. Some things become the floor's version of
themselves (a carried-in gin and tonic arrives on Varrow as small beer, floor-bound); others pass
unchanged, apparently on the tower's whim (the same drink in Kenzie's satchel stayed gin). BABEL
only tagged a few items floor-bound; the tower does it broadly. An Engineer's Floor Adapter can
carry one item through one transition.

## Floor clocks

Floors keep their own clocks. The Gilded Bazaar's dome cycles twice for every day on the
Concourse, so its nights are short and it never closes; the Marches and Varrow run day for day
with the Concourse. The rest are unknown. The engine runs one story clock, so this is for the
narrator and for off-page beats: two Bazaar days pass while a Varrow day does.

## Natives and climbers

Every floor has natives, who were born there, live there and die there, with a lesser, cruder
System (Brother Coll has Keeper Mend; Hamid has a nameplate). Climbers are the arrivals from
outside, from every era: veterans from before the Turning, a closed-alpha player from six years
back, the Halvorsen's wave. Climbers are the Unhungry: no need for food or drink, plain food tastes
of ash, and only System food (Waybread, Close honey, a Cook's dishes) tastes real.

## The Turning

About five weeks before the Halvorsen's wave, the tower changed skins. Before it, the System was
the Long House, with numbered levels and a Stone that greeted arrivals like a tent preacher. Now it
is BABEL. Every veteran was migrated onto BABEL's letter ranks with the Mabry curve, which is why
the Landing's nameplates read `Lv 14 ▸ Rank D`. Nobody has ranked past D since.

## The Concourse

Floor Zero, under a gold-veined dome so high there is weather in it, with bells hung in the haze
and the smoke of a thousand cookfires under them. A broad round plaza with a great white Waystone,
the Roll carved at its top and Nth Floor's maker's mark cut into its foot. Market streets packed
with stalls and banners; people in plate, in rags and in a Texas Longhorns hoodie. A row of guild
halls with banners faded past reading, the Landing at the end of it. The east gate onto the Stair.
A Safe Zone over all of it.

## Varrow

BABEL's starter floor, so close to the beta that it hurts: a river valley under a wide pale sky.
Hollin at the ford, its mill and stone bridge and green; Varrow Keep gray on its hill; the white
cathedral of Saint Aurel across the valley; hawthorn lanes; and at the valley head the Reachwall
holding back the Reach. The new thing is the board by the well: NO MARKED BEYOND THE FORD BY ORDER
OF THE CLOSE. The Close and the Keep share the valley uneasily under a compact older than the
Margrave. The Reach was an F-rank boar-man zone in the beta; in the tower it belongs to giants.

## The Drowned Marches

Fog, rain, brown water, reed higher than a man, black plank causeways on stilts and lanterns on
posts. Diseases caught here do not clear in a Safe Zone. Work is reed and nets. Notices come down
the chain from the Bazaar before the people they name do.

## The Gilded Bazaar

A merchant city under a lantern dome, where BABEL parked its high-end vendors and Jude Amsel's cut
pleasure-floor material leaked through in every build's flavor text. In the tower it is the floor:
rooms, salons and people are priced like spice. Sabeen Oro's Lantern Room; the Copper Scale, where
the walls are thin; the fence rows under the Copper Steps; the freight wharf on the shaft's edge.
