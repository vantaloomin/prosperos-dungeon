# The System

What arrivals see, what BABEL promised, and where the tower differs. In play the engine's Prospero ruleset
ruleset handles the dice; this is the fiction the narrator dresses them in.

## Creation

Arrivals wake bodiless in the dark before a pane set in Steward Sans. The Usher guides them through
race, class and name. Every pick is logged as a **request**; the tower decides what you are. It may
honor a request, honor it at a cost, or give you something else. Stats are read from watching you
hesitate, not rolled. Logout is unavailable, and there is no rig to pull off.

## MARVEL

| Stat | Governs |
| --- | --- |
| Mind | Spell power, perception, puzzles, speed of learning trained skills |
| Allure | Persuasion, prices, disposition, charm and seduction; scales desire-based effects |
| Resolve | Resisting fear, charm, pain and control; willpower; holding a Tether |
| Vitality | Health, stamina, strength, endurance, recovery (no separate Strength or Dexterity) |
| Essence | The energy pool for skills and spells; output and regeneration |
| Luck | Crits, drops, chance events, the odds of a Hidden class quest |

An ordinary adult starts at 5 to 15 in each. Around 30 is notable at F; around 100 is S-rank
territory. Dungeon's Prospero ruleset runs 1 to 30 with 10 as an ordinary adult, so starting sheets carry
over as they are.

## Ranks and death

Unranked, F, E, D, C, B, A, S, SS, SSS, Ultra. One XP bar per rank on the Mabry curve. A full bar
unlocks a solo Rank Trial at a Waystone (it must be unlocked there first); passing resets the bar to
zero, and a fresh rank is **glass**. Items use the same ladder. A first quest, kill or floor clear
takes you to F; discoveries pay XP too (finding the Long Stair, a maker's mark, the Muster Roll).

BABEL: die and respawn at your last Waystone, lose a quarter of the bar; at zero, permadeath.
**The tower:** lives are finite, hidden and only rumored. XP is not the buffer it was.

The package maps ranks onto Dungeon's tier ladder: Unranked to E are Local, D and C Regional, B and
A Realm, S and SS World, SSS and Ultra Mythic.

## Classes

| Starter | Role | Leads | Advanced from it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Breaker | Warrior | Vitality, Resolve | Berserker, Bulwark |
| Climber | Rogue | Vitality, Luck | Assassin, Scout |
| Speaker | Mage of spoken words | Mind, Essence | Hexer, Invoker |
| Keeper | Cleric | Resolve, Essence | Oathkeeper, Lamplighter |

Any line can be granted at E by behavior: Courtesan, Bard, Merchant, Diplomat (social); Smith,
Alchemist, Engineer, Cook (crafting); Gambler, Warden, Medium (odd). Hidden classes come from quest
chains: the beta rumored Oathbreaker, Consort, Graveclimber and Speaker of the First Tongue, and the
developers denied them all. Epitome is a single string in the build. **The tower:** classes are
earned by conduct; a Starter pick may not be offered, and a request can wait until you have earned
it (Keeper, honored for going to the Scales on your own feet, bearing weight that wasn't yours, and
asking before you bound).

## Skills

Three sources: class, training, and the System (granted for a Feat, a Pattern, something it
Witnessed, and hidden **Resonance**). Mastery runs Novice, Adept, Expert, Master, Grandmaster.
A sample of the library, from the Tower Ledger:

- **Trained:** Blades, Blunt, Polearms, Archery, Firearms (floor-bound), Unarmed, Shieldwork, Dirty
  Fighting; Climbing, Swimming, Stealth, Riding; Tracking, Foraging, Endurance; Lockpicking,
  Trapwork, Appraisal, First Aid, Cartography; Cooking, Brewing, crafts; Tongues; Persuasion,
  Haggle, Deception, Intimidation, Read Intent, Etiquette, Performance, Seduction; Sensual Arts,
  Pillow Talk, Stamina, Restraint.
- **Class:** Heavy Swing, Brace, Shatter, War Cry (Breaker); Quickstep, Grapple Line, Backstab, Pick
  the Seam, Vanish (Climber); Sigil Bolt, Ward Word, Element Word, Silence, Command, Name of the
  Thing, Babel Word (Speaker); Mend, Lamp, Vow, Absolve, Sanctuary, Martyr's Share, Last Rites
  (Keeper); and each Advanced class's three.
- **System:** Second Wind, Lucky Break, Unflinching, Cornered Rat, Liar's Tongue, Floorwise, Last
  Out, Kingslayer; Wayfinder's Instinct is Unique (Teo Ruiz holds it).
- **Resonance:** Kindled, Afterglow, Surrender, Dominion, Forbidden Fruit, Oath-Scar, Afterimage,
  Shared Skin, Sacrament.

**Commands** have durations scaled to the caster's rank; one laid through an object runs a third to a
quarter as long, and each reuse of a relic runs shorter.

## Resonance (hidden)

BABEL disabled Resonance XP. The tower didn't. Desire, taboo, emotional bonds, power exchange,
betrayal and broken vows pay power, and every payment has two costs: **corruption** changes the
user; **binding** ties or changes the partner. An engagement counts as a vow; breaking one can grant
Oath-Scar, a permanent bonus per broken vow that anyone can read on a full status pane. In play this
runs through Dungeon's dark progression tracks (`world.json` progression `resonance` and `binding`).

## Tether

In BABEL: an opt-in bond between two players, sharing stats and senses up to a 40% cap, designed by
Grant Mabry in winter 2023 and cut in spring 2024 after playtesters stacked Tethers to drain one
partner into another. **In the tower** it is live and older than BABEL:

- A Tether field wakes as a hook behind the breastbone that swings toward whoever weighs most in
  your history.
- A request lands on an **open** target (an untuned link and active desire toward the requester)
  whether or not they know. A mutual Tether, both partners agreeing with their eyes open, is rare,
  and the System flags it.
- A new Tether locks for about a day. Pulling against the lock tightens it.
- The **share** is weighed live by what passes between the partners: honesty, shared danger, touch
  and desire raise it; hurt and withdrawal lower it. Pain and sensation are shared at the share.
- A Command laid on one partner is carried down the line to the other.
- A mutual Tether is a vow in the tower's accounting, so dissolving it may read as a vow broken.

## Party links

A party shares telepathic chat. Until it is tuned it is unfiltered: stray thoughts leak both ways.
Words sent on purpose are written in italic guillemets; leaks stay in single-quoted italics.

## Panes on the page

Status windows and pop-ups are code blocks. Fields a character cannot read yet are garbled
(`▒▓░▒▓▒`), never left out. Natives' nameplates hang faintly over their heads.

```
GRANT MABRY · Unranked · XP 0%
Race ......... NEPHIL         [unstable · conflict]
Trace ........ WATCHER        [residual · ancestral]
Class ........ ▒▓░▒▓▒▒░       [request: KEEPER]
```

## The satchel and the Waystones

Every arrival's satchel: 25 Talents, Arrival Garb (fits any frame; absorbs what you came in
wearing, wallet and all), Waybread, a waterskin and a Starter Cache of six weapons, of which the
first picked binds. Attune at a Waystone to set your return point and confirm a class; around it,
a Safe Zone with no PvP and regeneration that heals injuries old and new, though not a rebuild the
tower left unfinished.
