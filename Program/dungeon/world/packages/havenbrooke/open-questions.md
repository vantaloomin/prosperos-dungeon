# Open questions and canon calls

## Decided by Vanta (2026-10-22)

- **The nine leads are playable,** and custom personas are allowed.
- **Real 2009 prices** in US dollars.
- **No hard lines** beyond Dungeon's adults-only rule. The world defaults to Unflinching for sex
  and Mature for everything else, and the player can change any of it.
- **The Companion modern Life deck** is Havenbrooke's base deck.

## Asked on cards, going with the recommended pick until Vanta answers

- **Nexus unit letters.** The lead bibles and the season notes disagree on who shares which unit.
  This draft follows the housing document and the floor plan: Unit A Piper and Jason, Unit B Rina
  and Austin, Unit C Kim and Michael, with Christine, Paula and Amanda in the singles.
- **Names that echo AI tropes.** The source uses Voss & Kincaid, Thorne-Ashford, Thorngate,
  Kaelen Hall and Maera. They are Vanta's own names, so this draft keeps them as written.

## Contradictions in the source and how this draft resolves them

- **Austin's role at Alpha Gamma.** His bible calls him secretary; the organizations document and
  Season One make him president, ceding to Dorian. Here he starts sophomore year as president and
  steps aside in Season One; Colm is the house secretary.
- **The Ember Circle and Kindling.** The source sometimes swaps them. Here Kindling is the student
  support group (from sophomore year) and the Ember Circle is the 1952 Greek society
  (Omicron Phi Ember).
- **Michael's housing.** One mention puts him in East Quad. Here he lives in Nexus Unit C.
- **Piper's goals** mention a capstone and senior year while she is a sophomore. Kept as her long
  plan, not her current term.
- **Hanae's tails.** "Five-tail tradition" in one file, three tails in another. Here she has three
  tails and comes from a five-tail tradition.
- **The Harvest Howl** is in September in the organizations document and at Halloween in Season
  One. Here it is at Halloween.
- **Vivian Corrigan and Vivienne Crane** read like one person. Here they are two different people.
- **Distances.** World_Landscape puts New York closer to Ithaca than it is. Here the travel map uses
  real distances (about four and a half hours by bus).
- **The leads' ages.** The prequel says they are 19 in summer 2009; the bibles give 20 in fall. Here
  they are 20 at the start of sophomore year, with birthdays left to the player.
- **The letter in Piper's tote.** From Jason in her bible, "the father letter" in the Season One
  notes. Here it is from Jason and stays unread; the twins' father's letter is a separate letter.
- **Freshmen's ages.** The four freshmen in the cast are 18 or 19, adults, and kept out of sexual
  content.
