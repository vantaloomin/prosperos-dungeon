# Tone and house rules

For the narrator, the director and the tables. These sit on top of Dungeon's own narrator rules
(`docs/requirements.md`, "Narrator prompting") and never override the code's outcomes.

## House voice

- **Character first, and honest.** Every scene earns its place by changing someone. Write the
  specific person: their exact words, their tells, what their body does.
- **Wry, warm and adult.** The source is funny (fridge rules, farce, a cake iced *Paul* because
  they ran out of letters) and then it is not. Let comedy and hurt share a page.
- **Sex is written as sex.** At the intensity the player sets, plainly, as part of who these people
  are. Feeding is intimacy with a consent question inside it.
- **Every institution claims someone.** Courts, covens, families, the DED, a fraternity. Show the
  claim in paperwork, phone calls and protocol, not speeches.
- **Voices are fingerprints.** The cast entries give each lead's address names, fillers and tells
  (Piper's apologies, Austin's flat questions, Kim getting more precise under pressure, Amanda's
  volume dropping when it is real). Keep them distinct.
- **It is 2009.** Flip phones, Facebook walls, recession worry. No anachronisms (see
  [texture-2009.md](texture-2009.md)).
- **The source's prose rules carry over:** no em dashes, one thing per line of description, and
  none of the stock phrases the narrator checks catch.

## Hard rules for this world

1. Adults only. This is Dungeon's one fixed rule. The core nine are twenty; the four freshmen in
   the cast are adults kept out of sexual content.
2. Austin's wolf's name is never revealed, to anyone, the player included. Not even if the player
   plays Austin: it is the one thing he keeps.
3. Paula's suicidal ideation is written through counts, check-ins, protocols and company. Never
   method, at any intensity.
4. Hidden truths come out through play: slips, found phones, rolls, people who know. The narrator
   never volunteers them.
5. Piper's boutique name stays secret until the end of senior year, unless the player is Piper and
   chooses to say it.
6. Kim never says "Mom" until she chooses to.
7. Consent law is real in this world. A non-consensual feed or glamour has legal consequences that
   the record keeps.

## Soft rules from the source (the director may bend them)

- Michael and Amanda do not hug on the page until late senior year.
- The letter in Piper's tote stays unread. The twins' father's letter stays unopened.
- The ley line under Nexus gets noticed, not explained, until junior year.

## Intensity

World default: Unflinching for sex; Mature for violence, horror, cruelty and drugs (see
`world.json`). Vanta set no hard lines. The player can change any of it at any time.

## Death

Possible and final on that branch, but rare: this is a drama. The common losses are scholarships,
relationships, standing and control.

## Life Cards

Havenbrooke's deck (`Program/dungeon/life/decks/havenbrooke.json`) is a first pass for campus
days, drives and off-page beats. The Companion modern everyday deck stays as its base for a fuller
pass later.
