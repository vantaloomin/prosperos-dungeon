# Campaign eras

Vanta's source is a prequel and three seasons, one per school year. The package offers each as a
place to start. Entries in the JSON files carry an `eras` list where they only hold in some of
them (missing means all four) and `by_era` notes where a fact changes.

## Summer before sophomore year (June to August 2009)

The prequel, "Terms and Conditions". The core nine are nineteen and scattered: Piper in her
father's Vermont town, Jason at his mother's in suburban Pennsylvania with Austin three doors
down, Rina at her family's grove, Kim under her mother's roof in Boston, Michael working full time
in Cleveland, Christine setting up her Ithaca apartment. Long distance is nightly calls after 9
p.m. on free minutes. The housing letters come on paper in cream envelopes and put three couples
in one Nexus suite, one door each. Nobody tells anybody, and everybody has the same news.

Starting here is light and charged. The radiator in the Nexus unit is warm in July, and the
housing steward on the phone is smoother than he should be.

## Sophomore year (fall 2009 to spring 2010), the default

Season One, "Naming". Fall term runs August 24 to December 18, 2009; spring term January 4 to May
14, 2010. A campaign starts on Monday, August 31, the first full week, with the boxes still not
unpacked.

What can happen: Piper's suite rules on the fridge; @HumanInterest launches; Kim's staged scene
collapses into farce; Jason founds Kindling; Callum Ashveil visits; the Harvest Howl on Halloween
Saturday; Dorian challenges Austin for the Alpha Gamma chair; a feed-swap with Vivian Corrigan goes
wrong on night two; a bitten lycan's scent in Ironroot Woods; Christine's Manhattan show and her
crash; Amanda's exposure-fatigue story; Michael's tattoo; winter break where nobody goes home; the
NSAC sparring bracket; Piper's proposition; Genevieve's layover; Mairead's stolen sealskin and the
search of Crane Hill; the spring equinox rite; a week at Seneca Lake.

## Junior year (fall 2010 to spring 2011)

Season Two, "Building". The iPhone 4 summer; Instagram launches on October 6, 2010. In the source
the circle is built this year, and every institution sends someone with a clipboard: an Eastern
Seelie auditor for Rina's forgeries, a DED review board after a feeding scandal, Kim's suite year,
a Crane who cracks the older sealskin. Deirdre's illness comes out at Easter.

## Senior year (fall 2011 to spring 2012)

Season Three, "The Cost". Occupy in the background, a job market still ugly for humans without
magic. In the source: Rina's hearing and severance, Paula's siphon coming out, Deirdre's death in
February, Kim and Michael ending without a villain, and the spring equinox re-anchoring under
Nexus that the whole series has been building toward.

## A note on the source's endings

The beats file holds the source's path through all three years, and the director steers toward it
only as hard as the campaign's hand setting asks. A player who changes things changes them. The
source's endings ledger (who wins, who gets something bittersweet, who loses) is one way the
story goes, not a promise.
