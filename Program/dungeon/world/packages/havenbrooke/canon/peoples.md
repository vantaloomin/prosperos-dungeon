# Peoples

The lore file has one entry per people for the narrator. This is the overview.

## The big four communities

- **Demonkin** (incubi, succubi): feed on emotional and sexual vitality. Warm, scented, vivid
  dreamers; glamours that leak. Some dreamwalk or partly shift; full shifts are rare and hidden.
  No national body: each line is its own law. The Vhen-Morrath, "line of the first hearth", is
  old and diplomatic.
- **Vampires:** a chosen transformation that runs on blood. Sunlight loosens them; day-walking
  needs jewellery or stored energy. Some bloodlines also feed on emotion. Born families run the
  twelve-seat Council of Bloodlines; turned vampires are tolerated, not equal.
- **Lycans:** born with the blood curse (it wakes in puberty) or bitten (a louder, harder first
  year). Dual-seat lycans feel the wolf as a separate person and often name it. The Lycan
  Assembly, founded 1952, spends its energy on born-versus-bitten politics.
- **Witches:** humans who channel magic through ritual tongues, diagrams and rites. Legally and
  biologically human. Organized in covens and lineages; the National Coven Registry in Salem
  keeps the books.

## The fae

Long-lived, elemental, bound by court law older than any human system, burned by iron.
Kitsune (fox fae, tails earned over centuries, emotional tethering, the bonded circle), dryads
(anchored to trees, vines, pheromone drift, betrothals), sylphs (air, voice, tether circles of
three to seven, marriages arranged decades ahead), selkies (the sealskin is part of the self),
leanan sídhe (muses who feed on artists), elves (six-hundred-year lives), changelings (taken and
returned), banshees (keening for the dying), pixies and sprites. The Fae Heritage Council speaks
for them to human government without ever claiming to speak for the courts.

## Everyone else

Nephilim (moral perception: they feel lies and cruelty), valkyries (death sight, battle
radiance), therianthropes (bears and others, blood-curse lines), beast-touched (ears, tails,
claws, fast processing), tengu, orcs (work-song magic, communal households), sirens (voices that
charm without permission), naga (venom with a euphoric compound), gorgons, merfolk, djinn (water
and fire clans, mutable edges, shaping intent), phoenix-blooded (a burning cycle, fire wards),
dragonkin (hoard instinct) and the godtouched (a fragment of a deity, and the weight of it).

## Mundane humans

Non-magical humans are most of America and a small minority on campus. They live with exposure
fatigue, the low drain of high magical density, and with being weaker than nearly everyone in the
room. Most come out of freshman year either deeply integrated or permanently wary.

## Vulnerabilities

Silver for demons and lycans; iron for fae and some witch lineages; sunlight for vampires; salt
water distance for selkies; distance from the anchor for dryads. Suppression fields (Thornewall,
the athletic wards) flatten everyone to their bodies.

## Words

Neutral: bloodline, pack, circle, coven, court, line, turned, bitten, half. Contested: mundie,
straight-blood. Slurs: leech, mutt, thrall, and "witch" as a verb for manipulation.
