# Havenbrooke

An adult supernatural university drama, set in the real autumn of 2009.

## The pitch

Vampires, lycans, fae, witches and sex demons are American citizens. They have been since the
Hartford Accords of 1787, and the federal government has regulated them since it founded the
Department of Esoteric Development in 1863. Four years later the DED opened a college for them in
Haven, a mill town in the Finger Lakes, on the spot where three minor ley lines meet. That college
is Havenbrooke University: about 5,000 students, a 20 percent acceptance rate, wards in every
building and Greek houses whose rituals are real.

Outside the gates it is the autumn of 2009. Unemployment is near 10 percent, everyone's parents
are worried about money, phones flip open, and Facebook is new enough that a lycan tagging herself
at a full-moon run is making a disclosure decision nobody designed for.

## What the stories are about

The source's own question is **who do you belong to, and who did you choose?** Every institution
in the world claims somebody: a fae court holds a betrothal contract, a Boston witch lineage holds
a daughter's future, a vampire family holds a town, the DED holds a licence and a review board.
The people at Havenbrooke spend four years working out which claims to honour.

The stories are character-first and explicit. Sex is written as sex, feeding is written as an
intimacy with a consent problem built in, and the psychology is honest: anxiety, depression,
bipolar disorder, grief and suicidal ideation are part of these lives. Good outcomes are not
guaranteed. In the source's own endings, some people win, some get something bittersweet and some
lose for good.

## The nine

Six sophomores share the Nexus third-floor suite over a ley node, and three more orbit it:

| Lead | People | Platform |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Piper Marsh | half-kitsune | Interpersonal Dynamics |
| Jason Kessler | incubus | Supernatural Physiology and Integration |
| Austin Hallward | born lycan, dual-seat | Military and Tactical Operations |
| Rina Aldwyn | dryad | Esoteric Sciences |
| Kimberly Corwin | human witch | Esoteric Sciences |
| Michael Reeves | mundane human | Mundane Studies |
| Christine Kessler | succubus | Interpersonal Dynamics |
| Paula Mercer | turned vampire | Interpersonal Dynamics |
| Amanda Rhodes | mundane human | Mundane Studies |

All nine are twenty and all nine are playable. A player can also bring their own character: a
freshman, a transfer, a townie, a professor, a Crane.
