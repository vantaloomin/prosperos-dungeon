# The campus

Havenbrooke University sits on the east bank of the Haven River. The DED chose the site in 1863
because three minor ley lines meet there, and opened the college in 1867 to house and educate
supernatural citizens after the Civil War. Oversight loosened over the next century, the 1954
charter reform made the university mostly autonomous, and scholarship pipelines now bring in about
300 mundane humans a year.

Students declare a major at the end of sophomore year. ES is the biggest and most competitive
department; MTO is the smallest (forty to sixty students), runs under federal oversight, starts
classes at 0700 and grades physical fitness. SPI needs two hundred supervised clinical hours,
mostly at Farrow House. Mundane Studies is required for anyone who wants a mainstream job, which
is why witches turn up in its finance seminars.

Courses named in the source: ESS 412 Emotional Resonance in Applied Incantation; IPD 340 Consent,
Power, and Feeding (mandatory for SPI and ES students); MTO 310 Containment Protocols; SPI 220
Feeding, Regulation, and Relational Practice. The school file the game plays from
(`engine/institutions/havenbrooke.json`) fills in days, times and rooms.

## Buildings

Teaching: Aldwright, Vespers, Thornewall, Kaelen, Warden, Bellweather, Farrow House, the Chancellor
Wynne Library and the Mirren Media Studio. Facilities: the Verdant Grove greenhouses, Stitch Hall
Ateliers, Evermere Health Pavilion, Crestfall Arena, Morrowe Commons, Caldwell Fountain Court, the
Old Observatory and Ridgewalk Chapel. Housing: North Dorm Ridge, the East Residential Quad, Nexus
Housing, the Farrow Annex, and the Greek houses. Grounds: the Quad, Lantern Way, Ironroot Woods and
the Old Cemetery. Each has a lore entry; the Haven town file places them for play.

## The ley lines and the wards

Vespers Hall stands on the strongest node, so ritual work there holds and recovers cleanly. Nexus
Housing sits on a secondary node: vivid dreams, poor sleep, a higher libido, and magic that works
better than it should. Wards are everywhere: energetic equilibrium in Morrowe, a suppression field
in Thornewall, aura stabilization in the library, sound dampening at Crestfall and Farrow, ritual
containment in the Vespers rings, a sisters-only boundary at Hex House, surveillance in Warden Hall
and consent detection in Evermere's feeding suites.

## Student life

Greek houses, clubs, the Senate, the Oracle, WHAV-FM and the Crestfall Crows are in the factions
and lore files. The Harvest Howl, the equinox rites, the charm exhibit, the Creative Collective's
spring show and the NSAC sparring bracket are the big dates.
