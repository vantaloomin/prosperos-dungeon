# Intimacy, feeding and bodies

A summary of the source's species physiology companion, for the narrator at the sex intensity
level. Everyone in this world is an adult. The source writes sex as sex; so does this package,
in fresh words.

## The principle

Every people's body changes what intimacy is, and most of those changes carry a consent question.
The source treats those questions as the drama, not as obstacles: who knows what is being taken,
who agreed to it, and whether the agreement was free.

## By people

- **Demonkin:** feeding is part of sex and can happen without sex (a kiss held too long, eye
  contact). Incubi run fever-hot and carry retractable spines at the base that release a mild
  aphrodisiac. A partner fed on feels warm, then drained; too long a draw means unconsciousness.
  Twins and close kin can practise the draw on each other.
- **Lycans:** they run hot, scent-mark, and a born lycan has a knot at the base that swells
  before climax. Drunk or aroused, the wolf comes up: teeth, eyes, scenting. Bitten lycans risk
  a shift during arousal until they are stabilized.
- **Vampires:** blood and, for some, emotion. Cool skin, no pulse to speak of; a feed in sex is
  common in established couples and legal only with consent. Evermere's suites log it.
- **Kitsune:** tethers make partners feel each other; a bonded circle holds several. The tail is a
  live readout of desire and is sensitive enough to stagger her if stroked wrong.
- **Dryads:** the pheromone drift rises with interest, and vines can bind a partner or the dryad
  herself. Extrusion bruises, which is why dryads wear long sleeves.
- **Witches:** magic follows emotion, so arousal can misfire a working; scenes are often built as
  deliberately as rites.
- **Sirens:** the voice charms, worst during honest moments, so a moan can entrance a partner.
- **Phoenix-blooded:** climax is a thermal event that can set off the fire ward; some edge for
  hours instead.
- **Naga:** venom with a euphoric compound; using it on someone unwilling is assault.
- **Djinn:** temperature, mist and touch reshape at will, and shaping intent can read a want
  before it is spoken.
- **Banshees:** sex quiets the death sense.
- **Mundane humans:** no powers, and the most legal protection; also the most exposure fatigue.

## Cross-species pairs

The source's favourite tensions: nephilim and demons (her radiance answers his hunger and her body
calls it attraction before her mind agrees), kitsune and incubus (the tether amplifies the draw),
dryad and lycan (the drift is strongest around a wolf, and she is small against him), succubus
and anyone she cannot steer, mundane and anyone at all.
