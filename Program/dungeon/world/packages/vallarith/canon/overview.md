# Vallarith at a glance

This page describes the default Post-Reform era. For the other two campaign eras, see
[eras.md](eras.md).

Vallarith is one continent and its islands, ringed by the Gulf of Aldir, the Dire Sea, the Kyrana
Channel, Sa'ja'een Bay and the Sea of Kujuur. The map calls it the Known World, which says
something about how far anyone has sailed and come back.

## What every adult knows

- **Magic is the Ley.** It is a current under the world, strong in some places and thin in others.
  You are born able to touch it or you learn to, and either way it does not care who you are.
  Ley crystals, mined from places where the current hardened into stone, let cities run on stored
  power where the natural flow is weak.
- **The Old Gods are gone.** Gor'gel of War, Morrigan, Hel, Oghma, Skadi, Hermod, the Dagda, Freya
  and Odur still get prayers, oaths and curses. Nobody agrees on whether they are dead, sleeping,
  or simply finished with us.
- **The New Gods are young.** Seven mortals who climbed into godhood at a Ley Font during the Demon
  Invasion: Nicana of Light, Aveus of Darkness, Cybelia of the Ley, Tidum of the Wild, Witarr of the
  Afterlife and Girione of Mortals, plus a seventh that mortals have never heard of. Their shrines
  are new, their priesthoods argue, and their miracles are uneven.
- **Dragons walk in human skins.** Everyone has heard that the Eternal Dragons live among mortals.
  Hardly anyone has met one knowingly, and the ones who have are careful about saying so.
- **The world was remade once.** In 3136 the New Gods ended the Demon Invasion with the
  Reformation. The old world broke and a new one stood in its place. Relics that survived it are
  priced like saints' bones.
- **Hell is real and it writes contracts.** Devils come with paperwork and patience; demons come
  hungry. Both have walked Vallarith's roads within the memory of the long-lived.

## The shape of the present (Post-Reform, about 300 RFM)

- **Cyrel and Asania are at war,** as they usually are, and Cyrel also holds the Badlands border
  at Bastion against the orc, gnoll and goblin tribes who took the fortress of Adlmyr from it.
- **Trein is the city of magic,** and the Brotherhood of Man, which once dammed the Ley and
  rationed it to licensed mortals, has quietly risen there again under Mistress Sara.
- **The Dwarven Empire** holds the north from Dun Aldun, sells the world its enchanted steel, and
  shares its mountains with a black dragon's swamp-cult at Rulan Gaan.
- **The elves keep to themselves.** The high elves of Brynwyr fight Cyrel's loggers for every tree;
  the wood elves of Petrakyra trade only with the crystal-rich Obirykan City States across the
  channel.
- **The south belongs to the sea and the swamp.** Goblin seafarers of Kadragar, the strange peoples
  of Bashrat Island, and the quiet monks of Ikto Isle.

## What the world is really about

Power in Vallarith always comes from somewhere, and someone downstream always pays for it. Dragons
want back what the gods took. The gods are new at the job. The Brotherhood wants control and calls
it freedom. Hell wants its king back, and its king is buried somewhere under the player's feet.
